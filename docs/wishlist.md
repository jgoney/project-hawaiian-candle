# Wishlist: the 3D game inspired by Road Rash

A running list for when the port is done and the new game begins. Nothing
here is planned or promised. "Owner" marks ideas the owner raised; "idea"
marks suggestions not yet discussed.

## Raised so far (owner)

- A real 3D world, in a look that matches the 2D original.
- Lighting and shading effects.
- New tracks and levels.
- Weapons.
- New game modes.
- Online multiplayer.
- Optionally smarter opponents.
- Own sound design (the owner's field).
- Release as its own thing: own name, own art, tracks, sound and tuning,
  with the driving feel carried over in rewritten code.

## Feel, carried over from the port work

- Keep the original's "deliberate" motion. The owner noticed at 200% speed
  that faster updates make the animation jittery, and that the original's
  slow update rate smooths the driving. Whatever the frame rate, the feel
  should stay.
- A softer far distance. Objects drawn from their full pictures look
  almost too crisp far away; some blur or haze with distance, or motion
  blur, is wanted.

- Lighting moods for the "hi fi" version. A throwaway experiment on
  2026-11-12 (branch `spike/mood-looks`, L key: golden hour, dusk, night
  ride with a headlight) was not usable on the 2D picture, but the owner
  found it "very encouraging" for the later version, where real depth and
  lighting exist.

## Ideas not yet discussed

- **Sound:** engine sound synthesised from revs, load and gear instead of
  samples; music that follows the race (position, speed, police).
- **World:** time of day and weather; dust, sparks and skid marks;
  headlights at night; traffic with more behaviour.
- **Riding:** a damage model for the bike; bike tuning and upgrades with
  real trade-offs; controller rumble.
- **Opponents:** riders with grudges and rivalries that carry across races,
  for every rider. (The original has this for one rider only, Natasha: hit
  her and she turns hostile, with a grudge that fades over three races.
  Side research of 2026-11-12, partly verified.)
- **Modes:** ghost racing against your own best run; split screen; a
  career with a map.
- **Tools:** a track editor; replays and a photo mode.
- **A "classic" switch:** original camera, original update rate and
  pixel-art look in the new world.
